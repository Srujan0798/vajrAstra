5. **surya is NOT CLEARED for a commercial product** — and the blocker is NOT the $5M cap we have been arguing about: it is a clause forbidding any product that competes with the licensor, with no funding or research carve-out. U14's framing was wrong. (RF-06)

7. **Bodhan hosting needs prior written approval**; its own card scores surya 0.1 on both mni and sat, corroborating that surya cannot serve those cells. (RF-08)
8. **Our script-identifier cannot route sat or mni** — it is a ViT, not CLIP, and its 12 classes exclude both. (RF-09)
